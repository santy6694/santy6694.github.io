When a company acquires a new brand, the clock starts immediately: marketing wants the new
site live, legal wants compliance content in place, and engineering wants it done without
technical debt. On a recent M&A onboarding project I used **Adobe Experience Manager Edge
Delivery Services (EDS)** together with AI tooling to compress a typical 6-week build into 3,
while raising the quality bar.

## What changed

- **AI-assisted scaffolding** generated first-pass EDS blocks straight from the acquired site's
  existing markup, which our team then refined instead of building from a blank page.
- **Parallel workstreams** were possible because EDS decouples authoring from the front end,
  so content migration and component development happened at the same time.
- **Automated review passes** (lint, accessibility, performance budgets) ran on every commit,
  catching regressions before they reached a human reviewer.

## The result

Go-live time dropped from 6 weeks to 3, with no increase in defects reported post-launch — and
the pattern has since become the default playbook for future acquisitions.
